SYSTEM LOGIC

Connected data flow

How one transaction updates its related modules.

01

Sales Order

Customer + specification + ordered quantity + unit + rate + delivery date.

Creates customer order history and production demand.
02

Purchase → GRN → Raw QC

Supplier, PO item, received/accepted/rejected quantities, unit cost and batch.

Accepted QC stock posts to raw material batch ledger; rejected quantity stays separate.
03

BOM → Production Plan → Job Card

Specification-wise BOM calculates standard consumption. Job card links sales order item, machine and planned quantity.

Material issue reduces raw stock and records batch-level issue.
04

Production Entry

Output, weight, batch consumption, material returns, scrap and operator split.

Draft does not post finished stock or labour earnings. Reconciliation is checked before approval.
05

Production Approval → Finished QC

Inspection parameters and accepted/rejected/rework quantities.

Only QC-approved quantity becomes available finished stock. Rejected quantity enters rejected/rework flow.
06

Reservation → Dispatch → Invoice → Receipt

Reserve batch-wise stock for order, dispatch partial/full quantity, invoice and allocate payment.

Finished stock, order balance and customer ledger update from source transactions.
